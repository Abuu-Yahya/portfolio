CoastTrack GPS

A mobile GPS tracking app designed around showing location clearly on a phone.

The goal is a simple experience for people who need to know where something is.

CoastTrack GPS is being designed around one practical requirement: make location information clear on a mobile screen. The interface prioritizes legibility and direct access to the map.

The project remains in progress; no public demo or repository is presented as complete.
