This page explains how information is handled when you browse this portfolio or contact me about a project.

1. Who runs this website

I’m Ali Abdinoor Muhammed, known as Bin, a developer based in Watamu, Kenya. For questions about information handled through this portfolio, email binabdinoor@gmail.com.

2. Information collected

The contact form asks for your name, email address, project type and message. If you email or message me on WhatsApp, I receive the information you choose to share through that service. Please avoid sending passwords, financial details or other sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

The website uses Google Analytics on pages where its script is included. The script currently loads automatically. Analytics can collect usage and technical information, such as page visits, browser and device information and approximate location. The contact form records a successful-submission event without including the message text in that event.

Hosting, form and font providers also receive technical requests needed to deliver their services. This may include connection information such as an IP address. Their policies describe their own handling of that information.

3. Why information is used

  • To respond to enquiries, discuss requirements and prepare project quotes.
  • To operate the website and deliver contact messages.
  • To understand site usage, improve the experience and reduce spam.

This portfolio does not provide visitor accounts or payment processing, and I do not sell enquiry information.

4. Services involved

  • Web3Forms: processes contact-form submissions for delivery to my inbox. See its Privacy Policy.
  • Google: provides Analytics, remotely loaded fonts and Gmail for enquiries. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • WhatsApp: opens only when you follow the chat link. Any subsequent conversation uses WhatsApp’s service. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

These providers may process information outside Kenya. Their policies explain their processing practices; this page does not promise a particular hosting location or transfer safeguard.

5. Cookies, fonts and browser storage

Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies. The website stores your selected light or dark theme in local storage. Google Fonts loads typefaces from Google servers. You can clear cookies and site storage through your browser settings; doing so can reset your theme preference. You can also use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. This website does not currently offer its own analytics preference control.

6. Retention

Enquiries may remain in my email or messaging history while we discuss your request and maintain related correspondence. This page does not specify a fixed deletion schedule. Contact me to ask about a particular message or request its deletion. Provider records and analytics retention depend on the relevant service and account settings.

7. Security and external links

Please use the secure HTTPS version of this website when it is published. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Links to Upwork, TikTok, Facebook and other sites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

8. Updates

I will update this page when the website’s information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

9. Contact

For a privacy question or request, write to binabdinoor@gmail.com. For a project enquiry, use the Contact section.
