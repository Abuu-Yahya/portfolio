Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. I have written it in plain language.

1. Who I am

This website is run by Ali Abdinoor Muhammed (known as Bin), a web developer based in Watamu, Kilifi County, Kenya. I decide how the personal information collected here is used, so I am the “data controller” for the purposes of Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019. You can reach me at binabdinoor@gmail.com.

2. What I collect

Information you give me

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the type of project you choose, and the message you write. The form service also records your IP address and the time you sent it.
  • Email: anything you send me directly, such as your email address and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics: I use Google Analytics to see how the site is used. It records pages viewed, how long visits last, scrolling, clicks on links that leave the site, file downloads (such as my résumé), the country and city your connection appears to come from, your device and browser type, and how you found the site. When someone sends the contact form successfully, it counts the submission but not what was written.
  • Hosting logs: the host of this site, GitHub Pages, may keep technical logs such as IP addresses to keep the service secure and running.

I do not run user accounts, take payments, show advertising or sell your information.

3. How I use it

  • To reply to your message, discuss your project and send quotes.
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful and to improve them.
  • To keep the site secure and reduce spam.

I rely on your consent when you contact me, and on my legitimate interest in running and improving a professional website.

4. Who else handles your information

I use a few service providers to run the site. They process information on my behalf or provide the services I use:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers contact form messages to my email.
  • Google, for Google Analytics, Google Fonts and Gmail.
  • GitHub, which hosts the website.

Some of these providers are based outside Kenya, so your information may be processed in other countries, subject to the providers’ own privacy terms and safeguards. I do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Google Analytics sets cookies that tell visitors apart without naming them. The site also stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage, so it remembers your preference. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s opt-out add-on for Analytics. The site will still work if you do.

6. How long I keep it

I keep messages for as long as we are discussing a project and for a reasonable time afterwards for my business records, unless you ask me to delete them sooner. Analytics information is kept by Google for a limited period set in my account.

7. Your rights

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act you may ask me to:

  • tell you what personal information I hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • delete your information, or stop using it for a particular purpose;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, which does not affect earlier use.

Email me at binabdinoor@gmail.com and I will reply as soon as I can. If you are unhappy with how I handle your information, you also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

8. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect the information I hold, including using trusted providers and encrypted connections. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so I cannot promise absolute security.

9. Children

This website is meant for adults and businesses. It is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Links to other sites

This site links to other services such as Upwork, TikTok and Facebook. They have their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for how they handle your information.

11. Changes to this policy

If I change how the site works, I will update this page and the date at the top.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email binabdinoor@gmail.com.
